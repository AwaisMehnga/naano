| Actor | Hotel analogy |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Guest booking a room |
| **Creator** | Hotel listing a room |

| **Naano ops** | Hotel staff who check in guests for them |

Company and brand agency both **buy posts**. Creator and creator agency both **sell posts**. Ops is **your team**, not a customer.

A login is always a **person**. The actor is the **workspace** that person is in.

## The five actors

**Company** — BlogSEO’s marketer. Searches creators, writes a brief, books a post, tracks clicks/leads, approves content so payout fires. This is the default buyer.

**Creator** — Thomas with 34k LinkedIn followers. Publishes a rate (€800/post), accepts a deal, writes the post, gets paid via Stripe. This is the default seller.

**Brand agency** — Same buyer tools, but **many client workspaces**. David does not run “Zmirov’s one campaign”; he runs Client A’s budget and Client B’s campaign separately. He is not a new kind of user; he is a buyer who owns several company workspaces.

**Creator agency** — Same seller tools, but **many creator profiles**. The talent often **has no login**. The agency sets rates, accepts collabs, delivers. A `creators` row can exist without a `user_id`.

**Naano ops** — Internal staff for the **€700 Managed** plan. They use the company’s workspace: pick creators, write briefs, run the campaign. Not a signup type on `/register`.

## What to put in the database

Do **not** do this:

```text
users.role = company | creator | brand_agency | creator_agency | ops
```

That breaks: one person in two agencies, a creator who also buys, agency-managed creators with no account, ops acting inside a client workspace.

Use three layers:

**1. Person** — `users` (already exists). Who logs in.

**2. Workspace** — the account that owns data (campaigns, billing, roster).

```text
workspaces.type = company | brand_agency | creator_agency | creator
```

**3. Seat inside that workspace** — what *this person* may do there.

```text
workspace_members.role = owner | admin | member
```

Plus a small **platform** flag for staff, not a customer workspace:

```text
users.platform_role = none | ops | admin
```

`none` = customer. `ops` = run Managed campaigns. `admin` = your superuser.

### How the five actors map

| Actor | Workspace type | User login? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company | `company` | yes | Members book/track |
| Creator | `creator` (1 profile) | yes | Owns their offer/payouts |
| Brand agency | `brand_agency` | yes | Parent of many `company` workspaces |
| Creator agency | `creator_agency` | yes | Owns many `creators` rows; those rows often have `user_id = null` |
| Naano ops | none | yes, staff user | `platform_role = ops`, then membership or “acting as” on the client workspace |

Creator profiles are a table, not a role:

- Self-serve creator: `creators.user_id` set  
- Agency roster: `creators.user_id` null, `workspace_id` = the agency
