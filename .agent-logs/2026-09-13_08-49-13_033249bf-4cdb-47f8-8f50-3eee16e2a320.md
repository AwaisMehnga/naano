A creator only sees a campaign if every step below passes.

**The campaign must be open to them.** Status is `active`, and they do not already have a collaboration on it (applied, invited, booked, etc.).

**Search, if they typed something.** Keep campaigns whose name or company name matches.

**Hard profile filter.** If the campaign has an ICP:
- industries/tags set → at least one must match the creator’s niches
- regions set → the creator’s country must match
- no ICP, or ICP with neither industries nor regions → keep it and let AI decide

**AI fit score.** Score the remaining campaigns (or reuse a cached score). Anything under **50** is dropped. If scoring fails, that campaign is hidden.

**Sort and show.** Highest match score first. Dashboard recommended is the same list, limited to 3.

Each card then shows company, location, audience relevance, match score, and deadline. Apply creates an `applied` collaboration the company sees under Invitations received.
